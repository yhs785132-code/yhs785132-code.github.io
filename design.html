About this site

Three sections unfold as you scroll.

A fictional academic profile by day; a collection of games and anime by night. The mode changes only when you choose it.

Design references

m4tt72 / terminal and Terminal theme
Compact monospace type, left-aligned links and a restrained accent color.
Jon Barron's academic website
A narrow reading column, ordinary headings, text links and research entries.
Steam Library
A title index beside selected artwork, with a shelf and game information below.
Fuwari
An illustrated banner, a collection sidebar and a readable article area.

CPU diagram

The home schematic follows the instruction-fetch and decode/execute organization in the lowRISC Ibex reference guide. It is a simplified logical illustration; the package and board connections are illustrative.

Design rules

  • Each page follows the structure of its content.
  • Fira Code for the home page; Verdana for academic text; Arial for the collections.
  • Use section names and work titles instead of slogans.
  • No fabricated statistics or ornamental labels. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
  • Artwork stays central. Controls stay simple.

Privacy

The name, education and projects are fictional. The site has no analytics, accounts or forms. Images and audio load from this site. Saved anime and the last night page are stored only in your browser.

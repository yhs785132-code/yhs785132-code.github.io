About this site

Three sections unfold as you scroll.

A fictional academic profile by day; a collection of games and anime by night. The mode changes only when you choose it.

Design references

m4tt72 / terminal and Terminal theme
Compact monospace type, left-aligned links and a restrained accent color.
Jon Barron's academic website
A narrow reading column, ordinary headings, text links and research entries.
Steam Library
A title index beside selected artwork, with a shelf and game information below.
Fuwari
An illustrated banner, soft surfaces, rounded controls and brief hover feedback.

CPU diagram

The home schematic follows the instruction-fetch and decode/execute organization in the lowRISC Ibex reference guide. It is a simplified logical illustration; the package and board connections are illustrative.

Design rules

  • Each page follows the structure of its content.
  • Fira Code for Home, Verdana for academics, Arial for Games, and Mochiy Pop One with Comic Neue for Anime.
  • Use section names and work titles instead of slogans.
  • No fabricated statistics or ornamental labels. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
  • Artwork stays central. Controls stay simple.

Artwork & music

The Academic background uses MOS 6502 die by Pauli Rautakorpi under CC BY 3.0. It is resized, converted to WebP and cropped for display. It illustrates the subject and is not a personal research result.

The game and anime images and four music tracks were supplied for this project. Rights remain with their respective creators and rights holders. This site does not claim an open license for them.

The local anime collection uses cover images and title metadata from Bangumi and bangumi-data. Each entry links to its source. Cover rights remain with their respective owners; the images are resized and served locally.

The reference sites informed the layout. Their personal profiles and illustrations are not used in the published pages.

Fonts & icons

The home page uses Fira Code under the SIL Open Font License. Anime headings use Mochiy Pop One (license); its body text uses Comic Neue (license). The interface uses Phosphor Icons under the MIT License. All font and icon files are local.

Privacy

The name, education and projects are fictional. The site has no analytics, accounts or forms. Images and audio load from this site. The anime collection is a read-only snapshot. Browsing it does not change the checklist. Only the last night page is stored in your browser.
